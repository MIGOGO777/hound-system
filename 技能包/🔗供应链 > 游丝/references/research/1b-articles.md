### 3.1 Reasoning Chain Template (10-Step)

| Step | Description | Supporting Sources |
|------|-------------|-------------------|
| **1. Identify Downstream Demand** | Start with hyperscaler AI capex (GOOGL/MSFT/META/AMZN NVDA) as the master leading indicator. Jensen Huang's projected $3-4T annually by 2030 is the top-down reason. | Methodology #1, X Article #1 |
| **2. Multi-Hop BOM Mapping** | Build the supply chain from capex → ASICs/TPUs → optical → epiwafer → substrate → feedstock. Every hop must be mapped explicitly. | Methodology #2, WOOK98 SKILL.md |
| **3. Find the Physical Constraint** | Identify the layer where supply cannot scale quickly (geological, chemical, manufacturing constraints). Distinguish quantity constraint from pricing power. | X Article #1, X Article #4, ChainCatcher |
| **4. Verify Monopoly/Duopoly Status** | Is the supplier sole/near-sole-source? No qualified substitute? Geopolitically constrained (China export ban)? | Singularity Research, ChainCatcher |
| **5. Assess Value Capture** | Is the component a small % of downstream BOM? (<10-15% = hyperscalers will pay through price hikes). Estimate pricing power and margin expansion. | Methodology #3 (ARR vs MC mismatch) |
| **6. Size Market Cap vs TAM Mismatch** | Compare current market cap against the TAM it bottlenecks. Small cap (<$3B) bottlenecking $15T+ of hyperscaler value = extreme asymmetry. | X Article #1, All SIVE/AXTI theses |
| **7. Check Institutional Lag** | Have institutions already accumulated (via 13F, OI, dark pool)? If not yet, the entry window is open. Morgan Stanley on SOI = validation. | Methodology #11, Singularity Research |
| **8. Apply GAAP Margin & Financing Filters** | Check real GAAP margins (not non-GAAP). Check for destructive dilution (large ATMs = disqualifier). | Methodology #5, #7 |
| **9. Apply Macro Tilt** | Current rate path, tariff regime, war risk — does it help or hurt this specific thesis? Algorithmic selloffs are entries; fundamental regime changes are exits. | Methodology #10, Singularity Research |
| **10. Enter with Conviction Tier & Catalyst Timeline** | Size according to conviction AND binary risk. Enter on qualification evidence, not revenue. Hold through volatility for LTCG. Exit when the chokepoint is resolved or priced in. | Methodology #13, #6 |
